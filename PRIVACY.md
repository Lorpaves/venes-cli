# Venes Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. [中文](#venes-隐私政策)

Venes is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that lets you follow and control the coding sessions running on your own Mac, together with the Venes CLI you install on that Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use them.

## In short

We don't collect your data. Venes has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your sessions go between your device and your own Mac, and nowhere else.

## Your sessions

The app connects directly to the Venes CLI on your Mac: over your local network, over SSH, or over your tailnet. The messages, transcripts, pictures and files you send or view travel only between your device and your Mac. They are not sent to us, and we can't see them.

Files you attach in the app are saved on your Mac, in its Downloads folder. Pictures and files from a session are fetched from your Mac when you open them, and kept on your device only while the app needs them.

The Venes CLI runs on your Mac, reads your sessions there, and checks GitHub for updates.

## Notifications

When a session finishes or needs you while the app isn't open, your Mac sends a notification through our relay to Apple Push Notification service. What the notification says is encrypted on your Mac with a key that only your Mac and your device have, so the relay can't read it. The relay receives your device's push token and the encrypted notification, passes them to Apple, and doesn't keep them. Your device's push token is stored on your Mac. You can turn notifications off in the app at any time.

## iCloud

The Macs you've paired, and the keys the app uses to reach them, are kept in your iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage and iCloud Keychain), so your other devices can use them. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy, and we have no access to it.

## Tailscale

If you sign in to Tailscale in the app to reach your Mac on your tailnet, that connection is handled by Tailscale under its own privacy policy.

## Camera, microphone and photos

The app uses the camera to scan the pairing code your Mac shows and to take the photos and videos you choose to send, the microphone for the sound of those videos, and your photo library to show recent photos you can attach. They stay on your device unless you send them to your Mac.

## Tips

Tips are in-app purchases handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports and analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage information with us.

## Children

Venes is a tool for software developers and isn't directed to children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: me@lorpaves.com

# Venes 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日。[English](#venes-privacy-policy)

Venes 是一款 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 应用，配合你在自己 Mac 上安装的 Venes CLI，让你查看和操控在这台 Mac 上运行的编程会话。本政策说明你在使用它们时，你的信息会如何处理。

## 简而言之

我们不收集你的数据。Venes 没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也不做追踪。你的会话只在你的设备和你自己的 Mac 之间传输。

## 你的会话

应用直接连接你 Mac 上的 Venes CLI：通过局域网、SSH 或你的 tailnet。你发送或查看的消息、会话记录、图片和文件只在你的设备和你的 Mac 之间传输，不会发给我们，我们也看不到。

你在应用中附加的文件会保存在你的 Mac 上的「下载」文件夹里。会话中的图片和文件在你打开时从你的 Mac 获取，只在应用需要时保留在你的设备上。

Venes CLI 运行在你的 Mac 上，在本机读取你的会话，并会从 GitHub 检查更新。

## 通知

当应用没有打开、某个会话完成或需要你处理时，你的 Mac 会通过我们的中转服务向 Apple 推送通知服务发送通知。通知内容在你的 Mac 上加密，密钥只有你的 Mac 和你的设备持有，中转服务无法读取。中转服务只会收到你设备的推送令牌和加密后的通知，转交给 Apple 后不会保留。你设备的推送令牌保存在你的 Mac 上。你可以随时在应用中关闭通知。

## iCloud

你配对过的 Mac 以及应用连接它们所用的密钥，会保存在你的 iCloud 账户中（iCloud 键值存储和 iCloud 钥匙串），以便你的其他设备使用。这些数据由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理，我们无法访问。

## Tailscale

如果你在应用中登录 Tailscale，以便通过 tailnet 连接你的 Mac，该连接由 Tailscale 按其隐私政策处理。

## 相机、麦克风和照片

应用使用相机扫描你的 Mac 显示的配对二维码，以及拍摄你选择发送的照片和视频；使用麦克风录制这些视频的声音；使用照片图库显示你可以附加的最近照片。除非你把它们发送到你的 Mac，否则它们只留在你的设备上。

## 打赏

打赏是由 Apple 处理的 App 内购买，我们不会收到你的支付信息。

## 崩溃报告

如果你在设备设置中选择与应用开发者共享崩溃报告和分析数据，Apple 可能会与我们共享匿名的崩溃和使用信息。

## 儿童

Venes 是面向软件开发者的工具，并非针对儿童。

## 变更

本政策如有变更，会更新此页面及其日期。

## 联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，请发邮件至 me@lorpaves.com
